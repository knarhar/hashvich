# Hashvich

Free calculators for living and working in Armenia, in Armenian, Russian and
English: take-home salary, sole proprietor tax, car import customs, currency
conversion at the central bank rate, and money transfer fees. Every rate links
to its source, with a quote and the date it was checked.

**Live site:** https://hashvich.lirare.cc

## Main sources

- [ARLIS](https://www.arlis.am/): the official legal database of Armenia (Tax Code and other laws)
- [State Revenue Committee](https://www.src.am/): tax and customs guidance
- [Central Bank of Armenia](https://www.cba.am/): official exchange rates, fetched automatically twice a day
- [Eurasian Economic Commission](https://eec.eaeunion.org/): EAEU customs rules
- Bank and transfer-service websites: fees and exchange rates for the transfer comparison
